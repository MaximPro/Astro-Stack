## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**StackBlitz not loading?**
- Try CodeSandbox instead
- Or use Codespaces

**Port already in use?**
- Click "Stop" then "Start" again
- Or change port: `npm run dev -- --port 3000`

**Dependencies failing?**
- Click "Restart Workspace"
- Or delete `node_modules` and reinstall
